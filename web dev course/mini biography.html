CLEOPATRA:

WAS ARGUABLY THE MOST FAMOUS FEMALE RULER OF ANCIENT TIME,RULING FROM BETWEEN 69-30 BCE

ABOUT HER:

CLEOPATRA WASFLUENT IN NINE LANGUAGES AND WASWELL EDUCATED WOMEN.

SHE WAS A BRILLIENT STRATAGEST BUT THE ROMAN VICTOR WROTE HER AS A "EXOTIC,DANGEROUS SEDUCTERES"

THREATS SHE DEALT WITH:

OCTAVIAN(32-34BCE) MASTERCLASSED A PROPAGANDA WAR AGAINST CLEOPATRA AND MARK ANTONY TO TRANSFER ROMAN REPUBLIC INTO AN EMPIRE.
THE EXPANDIG ROMAN EMPIRE WANTED TO CONQURE EGYPT ALONG WITH OTHER COUNTRIES BUT CLEOPATRA SUCESSFULLY MANAGED TO KEEP EGYPT INDEPENDENT FROM IT.
